FRAME
=====

INSTALLING
Open Frame right from this window. It moves itself to the Applications
folder and restarts from there. Without administrator rights, Frame goes
to the Applications folder inside your home folder. Once it runs, its icon
appears in the menu bar at the top right of the screen.

You can also install Frame by hand. Drag its icon onto the Applications
folder next to it and launch Frame from the Applications folder.

IF macOS WON'T OPEN THE APP
The app isn't notarized by Apple, so on the first launch macOS may show
a warning ("cannot verify the developer" or "Apple could not verify it is free
of malware"). You only need to allow it once.

  • On macOS 15 and later, close the warning with "Done", open System
    Settings, go to Privacy & Security, scroll down, click "Open Anyway"
    next to Frame and confirm.

  • On macOS 14, right-click Frame in Finder, choose "Open", then click
    "Open" again in the warning.

  • If that didn't help, drag Frame onto the Applications folder, open
    Terminal and run:
        xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Frame.app

RECORDING
A click on the menu bar icon opens Frame's panel. There you choose what
to record (the screen, a window or an area) and switch the camera,
the microphone and the Mac sound on or off. Start Recording or ⌥⇧R starts
a 3, 2, 1 countdown. While recording, a bar at the bottom of the screen
pauses, stops or cancels. After you stop, the editor opens: trimming,
zoom on clicks, a smooth pointer, the background, the frame and the camera
bubble. Export to MP4 saves the video into the recording's folder.
Recordings go to Movies/Frame, and everything stays on this Mac.

A right click or a Control-click on the icon opens the menu: recording the
screen, a window or an area, the recordings folder, settings, checking for
updates, About and Quit.

PERMISSIONS
On the first recording macOS asks for permission to record the screen, and
for the camera and the microphone when they are on. Frame cannot work
without screen recording. If a permission is off, open Frame's settings
(⌘,), Permissions: the Allow button opens the right part of System Settings.

LANGUAGE
Frame works in Russian and English. In Settings (⌘,) choose "Same as
System", "Русский" or "English". With "Same as System" Frame takes the
first suitable language from the list in System Settings > General >
Language & Region. A new language takes effect after a restart, and the
"Restart" button appears right under the choice.

UPDATES
Frame looks for a new version on GitHub at launch, every three hours and
after the Mac wakes up. It downloads a new version by itself, checks its
signature and installs it when Frame is idle. With "Install updates
automatically" turned off in Settings, the icon gets a dot instead, and
Frame's panel shows a card with "Later" and "Update".

OPEN AT LOGIN
Frame opens at login to install updates on time. To stop it, turn off
"Open at login" in Settings.

REQUIREMENTS
macOS 14 or later, a Mac with Apple Silicon or Intel.
